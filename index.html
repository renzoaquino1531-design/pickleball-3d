Pickleball 3D

Move with your mouse or finger. Your player hits the ball when it comes near: stand behind the bounce, and take it off-centre to angle it.

To choose the shot, signal just before the ball arrives. Phone: SMASH, LOB or DROP buttons. Computer: click or Space = smash, right-click or 2 = lob, 3 = drop. No signal = a normal shot.

Two on one device: near side uses arrows + Space / 2 / 3, far side uses WASD + F / G / H.
